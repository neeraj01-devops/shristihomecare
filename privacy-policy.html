Privacy Policy

How Shristi Care at Home respects and protects the confidentiality of your personal and health-related details.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Shristi Care at Home or request a callback through our website, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, physical locality in Gurugram, and details regarding the patient's non-medical care requirements.

2. Use of Information

All collected information is used solely to evaluate care requirements, assign appropriate caregivers, coordinate schedule timings, and communicate transparently with designated family members. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing entities.

3. Confidentiality & Security

We implement strict administrative safeguards to protect your privacy. Caregiver personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations regarding the privacy and dignity of every home they enter.
