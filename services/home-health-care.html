Home Health Care Support

Comprehensive, coordinated in-home healthcare support designed to integrate routine monitoring, recovery assistance, and personalized aid.

COMPREHENSIVE IN-HOME CARE

Integrated Care Support for Your Family

Managing healthcare at home often requires multiple facets of assistance: help with everyday personal tasks, monitoring of vital signs, structuring recovery after illness, and managing ongoing chronic health considerations. Shristi Care at Home integrates these services into a unified, stress-free care plan.

Serving households across Gurugram and Bhondsi, we work in close alignment with family members to ensure care continuity. Our dedicated coordinators review client progress, match experienced caregivers and nurses, and provide an unwavering pillar of reliability right in your home.

Services Included

Personalized Support

Flexible care structuring tailored around the individual’s unique daily rhythm.

Home-Based Assistance

Full support with mobility, personal hygiene, meals, and comfortable living.

Recovery Support

Attentive bedside assistance during convalescence and post-treatment recovery.

Elderly Support

Gentle companionship, safety awareness, and assistance with senior mobility.

Patient Assistance

Bedside routine, repositioning, and assistance with fluid & nutrition tracking.

Care Coordination

Direct communication and daily logging for total family transparency.

Notice: Shristi Care at Home provides supportive and home-health care services that work alongside your physician's clinical guidance. We do not provide primary medical diagnosis or independent clinical alterations to treatments.
