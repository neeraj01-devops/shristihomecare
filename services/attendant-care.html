Reliable Patient Attendant Care

Professional attendant support for individuals who require dependable daily living assistance, personal care, and comforting companionship.

DAILY LIVING ATTENDANTS

Dependable Support for Everyday Needs

When physical limitations make everyday tasks difficult, having a trustworthy and respectful patient attendant transforms daily life. At Shristi Care at Home, we provide compassionate male and female attendants across Gurugram and Bhondsi to assist with daily activities, mobility, and patient care management.

Our attendants assist with gentle personal hygiene, meal feeding, moving safely within the house, reminding the individual of prescribed medications, and maintaining a hygienic patient environment. Above all, they offer positive, warm companionship that brings peace to both the individual and their family.

Services Included

Personal Care Assistance

Support with washing, grooming, sponge bathing, and clothes changing.

Mobility Support

Assisting with sitting up, moving to the restroom, and wheelchair transfer.

Meal & Hydration Assistance

Serving prepared meals, assisting with feeding, and monitoring fluids.

Daily Routine Management

Structuring orderly waking, resting, and doctor-prescribed exercise times.

Patient Area Tidiness

Keeping the patient's immediate room, bedsheets, and utensils hygienic.

Companionship

Attentive conversation and continuous emotional presence throughout the day.

Scope of Care: Attendants provide non-clinical personal support and daily living assistance. For invasive medical procedures, wound dressings, or clinical injections, please request our qualified Nursing Care services.
