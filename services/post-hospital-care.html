Post-Hospital Care at Home

Dedicated recovery support after hospital discharge to ensure safe healing, rest, and routine adherence at home in Gurugram.

SAFE CONVALESCENCE

Bridging the Gap from Hospital to Home

Returning home from a hospital stay is an encouraging milestone, but the initial weeks of recovery are often challenging. Patients are frequently weak, disoriented, or require assistance with movement and medication schedules that families may struggle to provide around the clock.

Shristi Care at Home provides specialized post-discharge care to facilitate a smooth, comfortable recovery. By assisting with prescribed rest routines, medication reminders, hygiene, and safe mobility, our caregivers relieve the physical stress on families and help prevent avoidable hospital readmissions.

Services Included

Recovery Assistance

Supporting physician-recommended rest, hydration, and light activity schedules.

Mobility Support

Guiding gentle post-operative transfers and preventing sudden fall accidents.

Routine Care

Assisting with bathing, dressing, and comfortable bedding arrangements.

Medication Reminders

Keeping dosage times aligned with hospital discharge summaries and instructions.

Personal Hygiene

Careful sponge bathing and hygiene maintenance around surgical wound dressings.

Follow-up Support

Assisting family members in organizing discharge records and follow-up visits.

Important Medical Advice Disclaimer: Our post-hospital care services complement professional medical guidance and hospital discharge advice. Our caregivers and attendants do NOT replace treating physicians or surgeons. Always consult your doctor for medical treatment adjustments and emergencies.
